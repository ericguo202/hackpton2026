# Six Broad Criteria

- Delivery: Body language, confidence, eye contact, vocal tone and pacing — observable via webcam analytics and signals whether the candidate is poised and credible.
- Structure & Clarity (STAR/Storytelling): Use of a clear, structured narrative (Situation, Task, Action, Result) or other logical framing to make answers easy to follow and assess via transcripts.
- Problem-Solving & Decision-Making: Demonstration of frameworks, trade-off analysis, root-cause thinking, and clear rationale for decisions — visible in the transcript as stepwise reasoning.
- Impact & Results Orientation: Evidence of measurable outcomes, scale, metrics, and concrete results from actions taken — searchable in transcript and mapped to company needs via a brief overview.
- Leadership & Collaboration: Examples of leading, influencing, delegating, managing stakeholders, and working in teams — assessable from behavioral answers and interaction cues.
- Technical/Domain Depth & Rigor: Demonstrated role-specific knowledge, methods, tools, compliance or technical judgment appropriate to the function — visible in content of answers and depth of explanations.

# Technology, Product, and Design

- Emphasize structure & clarity plus impact: Product and design interviewers expect clear user-centric stories that end in measurable outcomes (user metrics, growth, retention).
- Technical/domain depth for trade-offs: Tech/product roles probe technical trade-offs (scalability, performance, design constraints) and expect candidates to demonstrate domain reasoning in transcripts.
- Delivery and stakeholder leadership: Confident delivery and evidence of cross-functional influence (engineering, design, PM leadership) are important and detectable in both transcript and webcam cues.

# Data, AI/ML, and Analytics

- Problem-solving with experimental rigor: Interviewers look for clearly articulated problem framing, hypothesis-driven approach, and experimental design described step-by-step.
- Impact & metrics-first storytelling: Answers should quantify gains (A/B lift, revenue impact, model performance) — transcripts must include metrics and outcome attribution.
- Technical depth & reproducibility: Expect discussion of methodologies, validation, bias mitigation, and production deployment considerations that reveal domain rigor.

# Cybersecurity and Risk

- Decision-making under pressure & incident handling: Behavioral answers should describe containment, communication, and post-incident lessons in a structured way.
- Technical/domain judgment and compliance orientation: Demonstrated understanding of threat models, controls, and regulatory constraints is assessed from detailed transcripts.
- Delivery & credibility: Calm, confident delivery and clear communication to non-technical stakeholders are critical and observable via webcam/transcript.

# Finance, Banking, and Private Capital

- Results orientation with quantitative outcomes: Candidates must cite deal outcomes, P&L impacts, savings, or ROI with clear numbers in their stories.
- Analytical decision-making & risk assessment: Transcripts should show trade-off analysis, valuation logic, or risk mitigation reasoning.
- Leadership in transactions & collaboration: Examples of leading deals or coordinating due diligence teams are weighted; professional delivery reinforces credibility.

# Consulting and Professional Services

- Structured problem-solving & frameworks: Clear, stepwise frameworks and crisp storytelling (STAR-like) are expected and directly assessed from transcripts.
- Impact and client-facing leadership: Candidates must show measurable client outcomes and examples of client influence or stakeholder alignment.
- Delivery & communication precision: Polished delivery, concise answers, and strong eye contact convey client-ready gravitas.

# Legal, Compliance, and Advocacy

- Technical/domain depth in judgment and ethics: Answers must show legal reasoning, precedent/application of rules, and risk-aware decisions from transcripts.
- Clear advocacy and stakeholder communication: Demonstrated ability to explain legal positions to non-lawyers and influence outcomes is crucial.
- Delivery and precision: Precise language, confidence, and professional demeanor (observable on webcam/transcript) are essential.

# Government and Public Sector

- Adherence to process & public-value impact: Behavioral examples should show process compliance, accountability, and measurable public outcomes.
- Stakeholder management & cross-agency collaboration: Interviewers look for examples of managing broad stakeholder sets and navigating constraints.
- Delivery with transparency: Clear, composed delivery and candor in responses signal suitability for public roles.

# Healthcare and Life Sciences

- Clinical/scientific rigor and patient-safety focus: Transcripts should demonstrate evidence-based decisions, protocol adherence, and safety-first outcomes.
- Collaboration across disciplines & measurable outcomes: Examples of cross-functional teamwork (clinicians, researchers, regulatory) and improved health metrics matter.
- Composed delivery under stress: Confident, calm communication in scenarios involving patient or trial-critical stakes is assessed.

# Sales, Marketing, and Customer Functions

- Impact and quota/campaign metrics: Interviewers expect explicit metrics (revenue, conversion lift, retention) and clear attribution of results.
- Persuasion, stakeholder influence & resilience: Stories should show negotiation, win-back, and resilience after losses.
- Delivery and charisma: Strong delivery, eye contact, and vocal energy (observable via webcam/audio) are weighed heavily.

# Operations, Supply Chain, and Manufacturing

- Execution-focused problem solving: Demonstrated ability to diagnose bottlenecks, implement process improvements, and describe operational metrics (lead time, OEE, cost reduction).
- Leadership in cross-functional execution: Examples of coordinating suppliers, manufacturing, and logistics teams are important.
- Technical/domain depth in methods: Use of Lean/Six Sigma or supply-chain modeling described in detail signals competence.

# Retail, Hospitality, and Service

- Customer-centric problem solving & service recovery: Behavioral stories should highlight empathy, fast resolution, and measurable improvements in satisfaction.
- Results orientation on throughput and satisfaction metrics: Transcripts that cite KPIs (NPS, CSAT, time-to-resolution) are strong.
- Delivery under pressure and personable demeanor: Warm, composed webcam delivery and positive body language are critical.

# Nonprofit, NGO, and Social Impact

- Impact measurement and resourceful execution: Case examples should focus on measurable social outcomes, cost-effectiveness, and creative resource use.
- Stakeholder engagement & partnership building: Demonstrated ability to mobilize donors, governments, or communities appears in transcripts.
- Authentic delivery and mission-aligned conviction: Delivery that conveys sincerity and clear commitment is important and observable.

# Education and EdTech

- Learning outcomes and pedagogical reasoning: Interview transcripts should show how interventions improved learning metrics or student outcomes.
- Cross-functional collaboration with educators: Examples of co-design with teachers, curriculum impacts, or measurement of retention matter.
- Clear, patient delivery: Communication style suitable for educators/learners, observable via webcam, is evaluated.

# Engineering (Non-Software)

- Systems thinking and safety/quality-focused problem solving: Behavioral answers should show design trade-offs, safety considerations, and testing/validation approaches.
- Execution & project delivery metrics: Examples that cite schedule, budget adherence, reliability improvements are important.
- Technical rigor and methodical explanation: Depth of domain reasoning in transcripts (calculations, standards, specs) is expected.

# Startups and High-Growth Environments

- Bias to action, ownership & adaptability: Stories should show rapid iterations, ownership across roles, and learning from failure.
- Impact orientation with early-stage metrics: Candidates must demonstrate measurable, often scrappy, impact and speed of execution.
- Energetic delivery and resilience: High-energy delivery, clear confidence on webcam, and examples of thriving under ambiguity are assessed.
